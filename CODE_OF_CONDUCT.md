# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity. Be considerate, give and receive constructive feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the project, and show empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention, intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner, Harsh Vishwakarma, through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments or commits, close issues or PRs, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
